<hr>

<h2>📌 Project Overview</h2>

<p>
This project is an interactive <b>Sales Dashboard</b> developed in Microsoft Excel for a supermarket shop.
The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using
<b>Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, KPIs, Conditional Formatting, and interactive filters.</b>
</p>

<p>
The main purpose of this dashboard is to help business owners and management quickly understand
sales performance, profitability, product performance, category contribution, payment methods,
and sales trends over different time periods.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🎯 Project Objectives</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Analyze overall sales performance.</li>
  <li>Monitor total sales and total profit.</li>
  <li>Calculate and track profit percentage.</li>
  <li>Identify top-performing products.</li>
  <li>Compare different product categories.</li>
  <li>Analyze sales by payment mode.</li>
  <li>Compare different sales types.</li>
  <li>Analyze monthly and daily sales trends.</li>
  <li>Create an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.</li>
  <li>Present important business KPIs in a single-page dashboard.</li>
</ul>

<h2>📊 Dashboard Components</h2>

<h3>1. 💰 Total Sales</h3>

<p>
The <b>Total Sales</b> KPI displays the overall revenue generated from all sales transactions.
It provides management with an immediate view of the business's sales performance.
</p>

<h3>2. 📈 Total Profit</h3>

<p>
The <b>Total Profit</b> KPI represents the total profit generated from the sales.
This helps evaluate the financial performance of the business.
</p>

<h3>3. 🐷 Profit Percentage</h3>

<p>
The <b>Profit %</b> KPI provides a quick view of profitability relative to sales.
It helps management understand how efficiently sales are being converted into profit.
</p>

<h3>4. 🏆 Top Product</h3>

<p>
The <b>Top Product</b> section identifies the product generating the highest sales amount.
This can help the business identify products that are performing particularly well.
</p>

<h3>5. 🏅 Top Category</h3>

<p>
The <b>Top Category</b> section identifies the category contributing the highest sales.
This provides a quick understanding of which category has the strongest performance.
</p>

<hr>

<h2>🎛️ Interactive Filters</h2>

<p>
The dashboard contains interactive filters and slicers that allow users to dynamically
change the displayed information.
</p>

<h3>📅 Year Filter</h3>

<p>
The Year filter allows users to analyze sales performance for a specific year.
</p>

<h3>📆 Month Filter</h3>

<p>
The Month filter allows users to analyze sales performance for individual months.
This makes it easier to identify monthly trends and seasonal changes.
</p>

<h3>💳 Payment Mode Filter</h3>

<p>
The Payment Mode filter allows users to compare different payment methods such as:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Cash</li>
  <li>Online</li>
</ul>

<h3>🛒 Sales Type Filter</h3>

<p>
The Sales Type filter allows users to compare different sales channels, including:
</p>

<ul>
  <li>Direct Sales</li>
  <li>Online</li>
  <li>Wholesaler</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2>📉 Charts & Visualizations</h2>

<h3>📊 Monthly Sales & Profit</h3>

<p>
The monthly visualization compares <b>Sales</b> and <b>Profit</b> across different months.
It helps identify periods of strong or weak business performance.
</p>

<h3>📦 Product Performance</h3>

<p>
The Product chart displays sales performance across different products.
This allows users to identify high-performing and low-performing products.
</p>

<h3>🥧 Sales Type Analysis</h3>

<p>
The Sales Type pie chart shows the contribution of different sales channels.
It provides an easy way to compare Direct Sales, Online, and Wholesale sales.
</p>

<h3>📈 Daily Sales Trend</h3>

<p>
The Daily Sales chart represents sales performance across individual days.
It helps identify daily fluctuations, peaks, and low-sales periods.
</p>

<h3>💳 Payment Mode Analysis</h3>

<p>
The Payment Mode chart compares sales transactions made through different payment methods,
such as Cash and Online payments.
</p>

<h3>🗂️ Category Analysis</h3>

<p>
The Category visualization compares sales contribution among different product categories.
It makes it easier to identify the most valuable categories.
</p>

<h2>🧹 Data Analysis Process</h2>

<p>The dashboard was developed through the following data analysis workflow:</p>

<ol>
  <li><b>Data Collection</b> – Gathered supermarket sales transaction data.</li>

  <li><b>Data Cleaning</b> – Checked and prepared the dataset for analysis.</li>

  <li><b>Data Organization</b> – Structured sales information into meaningful fields.</li>

  <li><b>Data Analysis</b> – Used Excel formulas and Pivot Tables to summarize the data.</li>

  <li><b>KPI Creation</b> – Created important business metrics such as Total Sales and Total Profit.</li>

  <li><b>Visualization</b> – Created charts to represent important sales insights.</li>

  <li><b>Dashboard Design</b> – Combined KPIs, charts, filters, and visual elements into a single dashboard.</li>

  <li><b>Interactivity</b> – Added slicers and filters for dynamic analysis.</li>
</ol>
